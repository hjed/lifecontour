*This summary is for convenience only. It is not part of the agreement, and the agreement's terms
below prevail over it.*

- **The licence is broad.** It is permanent, worldwide, free of charge and cannot be withdrawn. It
  includes the right to **relicense** your contribution under any terms, including licences other
  than the AGPL. Today the project is released under `AGPL-3.0-or-later` with the additional
  permissions in [LICENSE-EXCEPTIONS.md](LICENSE-EXCEPTIONS.md).

  project.
- **You confirm that you are allowed to contribute.** The work is yours, or you have the right to
  submit it. If your employer has rights in it, they have agreed.
- **No warranty.** You provide the contribution "as is", and you do not have to support it.
- **You sign once,** through the CLA Assistant bot on your first pull request. It covers all your
  future contributions to this project.

This agreement is not legal advice. If you are unsure whether you can agree to it, ask a lawyer
before you contribute.
