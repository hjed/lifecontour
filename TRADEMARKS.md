- **Run, study and modify the code** under the AGPL, including for self-hosting.
- **Redistribute unmodified copies** under the Lifecontour name, for example by packaging or
  mirroring an official release.
- **Say truthfully where your work comes from**: "based on Lifecontour", "a fork of Lifecontour"
  or "compatible with Lifecontour".
- **Refer to the project by name** in articles, talks, reviews and documentation.

## What you must not do

- **Distribute or host a modified version under the Lifecontour name or logo.** A fork must use
  its own name and logo. It must also remove the Lifecontour logo from its app icons and
  favicons.
- **Suggest endorsement:** do not use the name or logo in a way that implies your project,
  product or service is official or endorsed by the Lifecontour project.
- **Use a confusingly similar name or logo** for a related product.
